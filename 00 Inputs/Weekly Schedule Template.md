# My Standard Weekly Schedule
> Escalation Media · Weekly OS v2.0 · 8h Work System

---

- 6:00 AM: Wake up — hydrate, get ready
- 6:15 AM: Gym (1h 30min session)
- 7:45 AM: Shower + Breakfast (45 min)
- 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM: Deep Work Block 1 — Editing, production, creative output (4h) · NO emails, no client comms
- 12:30 PM: Lunch (cook + eat · 45 min)
- 1:15 PM – 5:15 PM: Deep Work Block 2 — Ops, outreach, client comms, Notion (4h)
- 5:15 PM: Personal time — dinner, decompress, girlfriend/friends
- 9:00 PM: Wind down — no screens, read, prep for tomorrow
- 9:50 PM: Meditation (5–10 min)
- 10:00 PM: Lights out

---

## Rules

1. **Block 1 = Creation Only** — Zero emails, Slack, or client messages until Block 2
2. **Gym is Locked In** — Mon / Wed / Fri, 6:15 AM. Non-negotiable.
3. **10 PM Hard Cutoff** — Screens off by 9 PM, in bed by 10
4. **Breaks Are Real** — Lunch is not a working lunch
5. **Weekend is Sacred** — Saturday fully protected. Sunday prep optional, max 2h
6. **No Meetings Before Noon** — All calls live in Block 2
7. **Client & Interview Calls** — Tue/Thu, 1–3 PM only
8. **Editor Comms = Async First** — Send briefs at end of Block 2 (4–5 PM). Vietnam (+11h), India (+9.5h). Review their output at start of Block 1 next day.
9. **ALVION & Networking** — Late Block 2, 3–5 PM. After client/production work, not instead of.

## Weekly Totals
- Work: 40h / week (8h × 5 days)
- Gym: 3× per week (Mon, Wed, Fri)
- Sleep: 8h / night
- Block structure: Mon/Wed/Fri = 4+4h · Tue/Thu = 5+3h
