Non-Negotiable Rules
Rule 01
Block 1 = Creation Only
Morning block is for editing, shooting, building, and thinking. Zero emails, zero Slack, zero client messages until Block 2. Guard it like money.
Rule 02
Gym is Locked In
Mon / Wed / Fri, 6:15 AM. Non-negotiable. Missing it sets a precedent for missing other things. It also protects your mental state for the work ahead.
Rule 03
10 PM Hard Cutoff
Screens off by 9 PM, in bed by 10. Sleep is the highest ROI activity in your schedule. Tired founders make poor decisions and produce poor work.
Rule 04
Breaks Are Real
Lunch is not a working lunch. Step away from the screen, actually cook and eat. This is the reset that makes Block 2 quality match Block 1.
Rule 05
Weekend is Sacred
Saturday is fully protected. Sunday prep is optional and capped at 2h. If you erode the weekend, burnout arrives faster than any client deadline will.
Rule 06
Run It for 6 Weeks
Don't adjust the schedule week to week. Commit to this exact structure for 6 weeks minimum. Consistency is what makes a routine into a system.
Rule 07
No Meetings Before Noon
All calls — clients, interviews, partners — live in Block 2. Block 1 is untouchable. If someone can only do mornings, offer an afternoon slot. Most will accommodate.
Rule 08
Client & Interview Calls
GTA clients and videographer interviews go Tue/Thu, 1–3 PM. Non-gym days mean you're not rushed from a morning session and your afternoon energy is at its best.
Rule 09
Editor Comms = Async First
Vietnam (+11h) and India (+9.5h) editors work while you sleep. Send detailed briefs at end of Block 2 (4–5 PM your time = their next morning). Review their output at the start of Block 1. If a live call is ever needed, 7–8 AM your time is the only viable window.
Rule 10
ALVION & Networking
Schedule networking calls and ALVION prep for late Block 2, 3–5 PM. Lower priority than client and production work — they come after, not instead of.
